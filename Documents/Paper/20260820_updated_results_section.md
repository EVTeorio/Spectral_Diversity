# Updated Results Section

## Results

### Spectral Heterogeneity Metrics

The three forward PCA-based spectral heterogeneity metrics were strongly related to one another, but they did not behave identically across scale. Standardized PCA alpha hull and standardized PCA mean distance showed the strongest concordance, increasing from r = 0.801 at 10 m to r = 0.904 at 20 m and r = 0.929 at 50 m. Standardized PCA mean distance and standardized PCA Rao's Q were also strongly correlated at all scales, with r = 0.907 at 10 m, r = 0.898 at 20 m, and r = 0.900 at 50 m. Standardized PCA alpha hull and standardized PCA Rao's Q were less tightly coupled, but their relationship strengthened with scale, from r = 0.553 at 10 m to r = 0.658 at 20 m and r = 0.722 at 50 m.

These patterns show that alpha hull, mean distance, and Rao's Q share a common spectral heterogeneity signal, while alpha hull remains the most distinct of the three. The strongest redundancy was between standardized PCA mean distance and standardized PCA Rao's Q, whereas alpha hull retained more independent behavior, especially at 10 m and 20 m.

### Biodiversity Metric Relationships

Biodiversity metrics separated into two broad groups. Species-diversity metrics were highly concordant with one another. Shannon diversity and Simpson diversity were nearly redundant across scales, with r = 0.969 at 10 m, r = 0.949 at 20 m, and r = 0.939 at 50 m. Species richness was also strongly related to Shannon diversity, with r = 0.824 at 10 m, r = 0.856 at 20 m, and r = 0.847 at 50 m.

Faith's PD tracked species richness strongly but not perfectly. The Faith's PD-richness relationship was r = 0.710 at 10 m, r = 0.727 at 20 m, and r = 0.761 at 50 m. Phylogenetic Rao's Q and abundance-weighted Faith's PD were also strongly related, with r = 0.750 at 10 m, r = 0.742 at 20 m, and r = 0.688 at 50 m. In contrast, abundance-weighted Faith's PD diverged from Shannon diversity with increasing scale: r = 0.426 at 10 m, r = 0.152 at 20 m, and r = -0.120 at 50 m. This divergence is important because abundance-weighted Faith's PD was one of the biodiversity metrics most strongly related to PCA spectral heterogeneity, while Shannon diversity was not.

### PCA Spectral Heterogeneity And Biodiversity Across Scale

Relationships between standardized PCA spectral heterogeneity and biodiversity strengthened with quadrat scale and were consistently stronger for phylogenetic metrics than for conventional species-diversity metrics. At 10 m, the strongest PCA spectral-biodiversity relationships were positive but weak. Standardized PCA alpha hull had the strongest 10 m relationships, led by abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.188, R2 = 0.035), phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.177, R2 = 0.031), and Faith's PD (r = 0.153, R2 = 0.023). Standardized PCA mean distance was weaker but followed the same pattern, with phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.140, R2 = 0.020), abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.122, R2 = 0.015), and Faith's PD (r = 0.119, R2 = 0.014). Standardized PCA Rao's Q showed little positive phylogenetic signal at 10 m; its strongest absolute relationships were weak negative relationships with species richness, Shannon diversity, and Simpson diversity.

At 20 m, the spectral-biodiversity signal strengthened. Standardized PCA alpha hull again showed the strongest relationships, especially with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.384, R2 = 0.147) and phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.336, R2 = 0.113). Standardized PCA mean distance also strengthened, with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.301, R2 = 0.091), phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.274, R2 = 0.075), and Faith's PD (r = 0.240, R2 = 0.058). Standardized PCA Rao's Q remained weaker than alpha hull and mean distance, with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.133, R2 = 0.018), phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.132, R2 = 0.017), and Faith's PD (r = 0.123, R2 = 0.015).

At 50 m, standardized PCA spectral heterogeneity showed its strongest relationships with biodiversity. Standardized PCA alpha hull was the strongest spectral metric overall, with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.497, R2 = 0.247) and phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.487, R2 = 0.238). Standardized PCA mean distance was nearly as strong for phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.444, R2 = 0.197) and abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.419, R2 = 0.175). Standardized PCA Rao's Q showed moderate relationships with phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.352, R2 = 0.124) and abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.272, R2 = 0.074), but remained weaker than alpha hull and mean distance.

The scale pattern was consistent across the three PCA metrics. Relationships were weakest at 10 m, stronger at 20 m, and strongest at 50 m. The clearest evidence for the Spectral Variation Hypothesis occurred at 50 m, where the leading relationships explained approximately 18-25% of variation in standardized PCA alpha hull or mean distance.

### Species-Diversity Contrasts

Species-diversity metrics showed weak, near-zero, or inconsistent relationships with the three PCA spectral heterogeneity metrics. Shannon diversity was the clearest contrast. At 10 m, Shannon diversity had near-zero relationships with standardized PCA alpha hull (r = -0.025, R2 = 0.001), standardized PCA mean distance (r = -0.032, R2 = 0.001), and standardized PCA Rao's Q (r = -0.060, R2 = 0.004). At 20 m, Shannon relationships remained weak for alpha hull (r = -0.024, R2 = 0.001), mean distance (r = -0.036, R2 = 0.001), and Rao's Q (r = -0.061, R2 = 0.004). At 50 m, Shannon diversity remained effectively unrelated to the PCA metrics, with r = 0.007 for alpha hull, r = 0.009 for mean distance, and r = 0.035 for Rao's Q.

This contrast was strongest at 50 m. At that scale, standardized PCA alpha hull related strongly to abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.497) and phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.487), while its relationship with Shannon diversity was r = 0.007. Standardized PCA mean distance showed the same pattern, with r = 0.444 for phylogenetic Rao's Q and r = 0.419 for abundance-weighted Faith's PD, but r = 0.009 for Shannon diversity. The spectral signal therefore aligned more strongly with phylogenetic composition and abundance-weighted evolutionary structure than with conventional species-diversity indices.

### Raw PCA Versus Vector-Normalized PCA

The PCA bases differed strongly in how much variation was concentrated in the first axes. In the raw PCA, PC1 explained 66.2% of spectral variation and the first two PCs explained 87.0%. In the vector-normalized PCA, PC1 explained 45.5% and the first two PCs explained 68.0%, indicating a less brightness-dominated ordination. The raw PCA brightness diagnostic confirmed this difference: raw PC1 was strongly correlated with mean reflectance at 50 m at both the pixel level (r = 0.893, R2 = 0.798) and quadrat-mean level (r = 0.925, R2 = 0.856). Standardized PC1 had much weaker mean-reflectance relationships at the pixel level (r = -0.118, R2 = 0.014) and quadrat-mean level (r = -0.348, R2 = 0.121).

The PCA loading diagnostics also separated the raw and vector-normalized bases. In the standardized PCA, PC1 departed most from a uniform brightness vector in the near infrared from 843-903 nm, while PC2 had concentrated nonuniform structure from 753-813 nm. The PC2 region least attributable to broad PC1 brightness was 753-778 nm. In the raw PCA, PC1 departures occurred at the violet-blue edge and longer near-infrared wavelengths, while PC2 emphasized blue-green, red, red-edge, and near-infrared regions. These diagnostics support the interpretation that the vector-normalized PCA retained spectral structure while reducing the dominant brightness axis present in the raw PCA.

Vector-normalized PCA metrics generally showed stronger spectral-biodiversity relationships than their raw PCA counterparts. At 10 m, raw PCA alpha hull was weakly related to abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.057), while standardized PCA alpha hull reached r = 0.188. Raw PCA mean distance was weakly related to phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.053), while standardized PCA mean distance reached r = 0.140. Raw PCA Rao's Q showed little phylogenetic signal at 10 m, similar to standardized PCA Rao's Q.

At 20 m, the vector-normalized advantage was clearer. Raw PCA alpha hull related to abundance-weighted Faith's PD at r = 0.217, compared with r = 0.384 for standardized PCA alpha hull. Raw PCA mean distance related to abundance-weighted Faith's PD at r = 0.164, compared with r = 0.301 for standardized PCA mean distance. Raw PCA Rao's Q remained similar to standardized PCA Rao's Q at this scale, with both showing weak relationships with abundance-weighted Faith's PD.

At 50 m, both raw and standardized PCA metrics showed positive phylogenetic relationships, but standardized PCA alpha hull and standardized PCA mean distance remained the strongest. Raw PCA alpha hull reached r = 0.412 with abundance-weighted Faith's PD, compared with r = 0.497 for standardized PCA alpha hull. Raw PCA mean distance reached r = 0.375 with abundance-weighted Faith's PD, compared with r = 0.419 for standardized PCA mean distance. Raw PCA Rao's Q reached r = 0.346 with abundance-weighted Faith's PD, compared with r = 0.272 for standardized PCA Rao's Q; however, standardized PCA Rao's Q was stronger with phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.352) than with abundance-weighted Faith's PD.

### Shadow Masking Diagnostic

The shadow/sunlit diagnostic showed near-perfect separation between training pixels. The implemented 563 nm band was effectively equivalent (AUC = 0.999956), with an optimal threshold of 0.0305476, sensitivity = 0.99931, and specificity = 0.99920. Supporting the retained sunlit-pixel mask as a very strong illumination filter rather than a weak or ambiguous preprocessing step.

### Metric Drivers

The three standardized PCA metrics were related to brightness, spectral-region brightness, and elevation. Standardized PCA alpha hull showed the strongest driver relationships among the three metrics. At 10 m, alpha hull was positively related to green brightness (r = 0.483), blue brightness (r = 0.461), and 563 nm brightness (r = 0.435). At 20 m, alpha hull remained positively related to green brightness (r = 0.332) and 563 nm brightness (r = 0.296), while also showing a negative relationship with near-infrared brightness (r = -0.366). At 50 m, alpha hull was negatively related to elevation (r = -0.485), positively related to green brightness (r = 0.471), positively related to 563 nm brightness (r = 0.432), and negatively related to near-infrared brightness (r = -0.421).

Standardized PCA mean distance showed a similar but slightly weaker driver pattern. It was negatively related to near-infrared brightness at 10 m (r = -0.352), 20 m (r = -0.357), and 50 m (r = -0.466). It was also negatively related to elevation at 50 m (r = -0.482) and positively related to green brightness at 50 m (r = 0.385). Standardized PCA Rao's Q had weaker driver relationships overall, but was negatively related to near-infrared brightness at 50 m (r = -0.389) and to elevation at 50 m (r = -0.318).

Biodiversity metric drivers also differed by metric type. Species richness was exactly aligned with present species count, while Shannon diversity was strongly aligned with Simpson diversity and species count. Abundance-weighted Faith's PD was strongly related to crown-equivalent individuals, with r = 0.718 at 10 m, r = 0.651 at 20 m, and r = 0.530 at 50 m. Phylogenetic Rao's Q was related to Simpson diversity and species evenness at 10 m, but its relationship with abundance-weighted Faith's PD was stronger and more consistent than its relationship with Shannon diversity. These patterns reinforce the separation between conventional species-diversity structure and abundance-weighted phylogenetic structure.

### Transformation Results

The biodiversity-squared transformation changed some correlations but did not overturn the untransformed rank order. The strongest relationship, standardized PCA alpha hull with abundance-weighted Faith's PD at 50 m, was essentially unchanged after squaring the biodiversity axis (r = 0.498, R2 = 0.248) relative to the untransformed result (r = 0.497, R2 = 0.247). Several 50 m phylogenetic relationships were slightly weaker after the biodiversity-squared transformation, including standardized PCA alpha hull with phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.474, R2 = 0.224), standardized PCA mean distance with phylogenetic Rao's Q (r = 0.425, R2 = 0.181), and standardized PCA mean distance with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (r = 0.408, R2 = 0.167).

At smaller scales, the biodiversity-squared transformation modestly strengthened some weaker phylogenetic relationships. At 20 m, standardized PCA alpha hull with phylogenetic Rao's Q increased to r = 0.345 (R2 = 0.119), while its relationship with abundance-weighted Faith's PD remained essentially unchanged at r = 0.384 (R2 = 0.147). At 10 m, standardized PCA alpha hull increased to r = 0.226 with phylogenetic Rao's Q and r = 0.220 with abundance-weighted Faith's PD. Standardized PCA mean distance also increased at 10 m, reaching r = 0.167 with phylogenetic Rao's Q and r = 0.147 with abundance-weighted Faith's PD. Overall, squaring the biodiversity axis mainly emphasized weak-to-moderate small-scale phylogenetic relationships; the strongest 50 m spectral-biodiversity relationships remained best represented by the original biodiversity values.

### Spatial Structure

The priority spectral and biodiversity variables showed spatial structure at all three quadrat scales. Standardized PCA alpha hull had Moran's I = 0.417 at 10 m, 0.318 at 20 m, and 0.221 at 50 m. Standardized PCA mean distance had Moran's I = 0.281 at 10 m, 0.263 at 20 m, and 0.214 at 50 m. Standardized PCA Rao's Q had lower but still positive spatial structure, with Moran's I = 0.178 at 10 m, 0.174 at 20 m, and 0.112 at 50 m. Phylogenetic Rao's Q and abundance-weighted Faith's PD were also spatially structured across scales.

Residual spatial structure was strongest at 10 m and 20 m and weaker for the strongest 50 m phylogenetic relationships. At 10 m, residual Moran's I remained positive for all priority PCA-biodiversity relationships, including standardized PCA alpha hull with phylogenetic Rao's Q (I = 0.394), standardized PCA alpha hull with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (I = 0.385), and standardized PCA mean distance with phylogenetic Rao's Q (I = 0.262). At 20 m, residual spatial structure persisted but was reduced for phylogenetic relationships, including standardized PCA alpha hull with phylogenetic Rao's Q (I = 0.228), standardized PCA alpha hull with abundance-weighted Faith's PD (I = 0.202), and standardized PCA mean distance with phylogenetic Rao's Q (I = 0.192).

At 50 m, residual spatial structure was weak or absent for the strongest phylogenetic relationships. Standardized PCA alpha hull with phylogenetic Rao's Q had residual Moran's I = 0.051, and standardized PCA alpha hull with abundance-weighted Faith's PD had residual Moran's I = 0.068. Standardized PCA mean distance with phylogenetic Rao's Q had residual Moran's I = 0.073, and standardized PCA Rao's Q with phylogenetic Rao's Q had residual Moran's I = 0.041. In contrast, residuals from Shannon-diversity relationships retained stronger spatial structure at 50 m, including standardized PCA alpha hull with Shannon diversity (I = 0.219) and standardized PCA mean distance with Shannon diversity (I = 0.212). This pattern supports the scale-dependent result: the strongest 50 m phylogenetic relationships also showed less residual spatial structure than the corresponding Shannon-diversity contrasts.
