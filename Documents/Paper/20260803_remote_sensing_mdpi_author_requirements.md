- Remote Sensing is an international, peer-reviewed, open access journal focused on the science and application of remote sensing technology.
- Publication frequency is semimonthly.
- Electronic ISSN: `2072-4292`.
- Publisher location: Basel, Switzerland.

- The journal is indexed in major databases including Scopus and Science Citation Index Expanded.

## Article Type For This Manuscript

Likely article type: **Article / original research manuscript**.

MDPI describes Articles as original research manuscripts. For Remote Sensing, Articles should:

- Report scientifically sound experiments or analyses.
- Provide a substantial amount of new information.
- Include enough experimental/methodological detail for results to be reproduced.
- Present Results and Discussion as separate sections.
- Generally comprise at least 18 pages.

## Word And Length Requirements

- **Main manuscript:** Remote Sensing states that there are **no restrictions on maximum manuscript length**, provided the text is concise and comprehensive.
- **Article minimum size:** original research Articles should generally be at least **18 pages**.
- **Abstract:** Remote Sensing instructions describe the abstract as a single paragraph of **about 300 words**.
- **Keywords:** **3 to 10** keywords.
- **File upload limit:** total data for all submitted files should not exceed **120 MB**. Larger data should use external repositories or contact the Editorial Office.
- **Large datasets:** files or datasets greater than **250 MB** should be deposited with specialized providers or institutional/subject repositories, preferably with DataCite support.

## Front Matter Requirements

### Title

- Concise, specific, and relevant.
- Do not include a short title, abbreviated title, running title, or head.

### Authors And Affiliations

- Full first and last names are required.
- Middle initials may be included.
- Affiliations should include complete address information: city, zip/postal code, state/province, and country.

### Highlights

- Optional for Remote Sensing.
- Should not repeat the abstract.
- Should briefly state:
  - Main findings, up to two bullets.
  - Main implications, up to two bullets.

### Abstract

- One paragraph.
- About 300 words.
- Must be self-contained.
- Define acronyms.
- Should include:
  - Broad context and study purpose.
  - Data used.
  - Approach and methods.
  - Key findings.
  - Strengths, limitations, or broader implications where appropriate.

### Keywords

- Include 3 to 10.
- Should be specific to the article but common enough for indexing and discovery.

## Formatting And Submission

- Remote Sensing accepts **free format submission** at initial submission.
- Microsoft Word and LaTeX templates are encouraged and should be used before final formatting/revision.
- Word manuscripts should be submitted as a single file.
- Graphics should be inserted in the main text after the paragraph where they are first cited.
- LaTeX submissions should be collated into one ZIP folder with all source files and images.
- Supplementary files may be any format, but common non-proprietary formats are recommended.
- Submission is through MDPI's online system: <https://susy.mdpi.com>.

## References And Citations

- References should be numbered in order of appearance.
- In-text reference numbers go in square brackets before punctuation, for example `[1]` or `[1-3]`.
- Reference list entries should include full article titles.
- DOI numbers are highly encouraged.
- Bibliography software such as Zotero, EndNote, Mendeley, or Reference Manager is recommended.
- References in supplementary files are allowed, but they must also appear in the main manuscript reference list.

## Data, Code, And Reproducibility Expectations

- Full experimental or analytical details should be included so results can be reproduced.
- Authors are expected to make datasets available where possible.
- Data, code, and other citable research materials are encouraged as citations.
- Avoid "data not shown"; observations related to the manuscript should be published as supplementary material when appropriate.
- Restrictions on data availability should be stated during submission and in the manuscript.

## Cover Letter

A cover letter is required. It should:

- Be concise.
- Explain why the paper is significant.
- Place the findings in the context of existing work.
- Explain why the manuscript fits Remote Sensing.
- Acknowledge any prior submissions to MDPI journals, if applicable.

## Fit Notes For This Paper

- UAV/drone hyperspectral imagery.
